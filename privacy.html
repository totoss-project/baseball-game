English

Number Baseball does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, or contacts. Game progress, rankings you enter by name, and settings are stored only on your device.

Game Center. If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Signing in is optional.

Advertising. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier) and usage data to serve and measure ads. On iOS you are asked for tracking permission; if you decline, ads are non-personalized. In the EU/UK you are shown a consent form and can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy options. See Google's privacy policy.

Children. The app is rated 4+ but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
